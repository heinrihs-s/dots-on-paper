# Asset provenance

The four plush dot illustrations were generated for the original Dots on Paper demo using OpenAI image generation. Their source prompt files and embedded PNG provenance travel with the package under `src/dots_on_paper/assets/`. They are reused unchanged here.

Figtree is bundled for offline rendering under the SIL Open Font License. Its license is `src/dots_on_paper/assets/Figtree-LICENSE.txt`.

The bridge renderer creates native grayscale/monochrome frames from these source illustrations. The standalone `demo/` and `campaign/media/` use staged copy, separate from live assistant replies. PNG/GIF exports embed rendering/source provenance; MP4 exports have neighboring provenance JSON. The film is illustrative animation, not a recording of physical e-ink refresh.

The project logo and icons are original vector artwork in `brand/`. The social preview combines that identity with a staged demo screenshot; its editable HTML source and provenance are included beside the PNG. These project assets do not imply an official product logo or partnership.

ChatGPT, Codex, TRMNL, and Instinct names identify the products discussed in the documentation. This is an independent project, with no claimed endorsement or partnership. The Instinct-inspired still is scripted concept artwork, and this package includes no Instinct connector.
